How this dashboard works

1. Upload data: Select your Sample - Superstore.csv file. The app reads the columns Order Date, Sales, Profit, Order ID, Category, Sub-Category, Region and Segment.

2. Filter: Choose an order-date range, region or category. All KPI cards and charts recalculate from the filtered rows.

3. Read the KPIs: Total Sales adds Sales; Total Profit adds Profit; Total Orders counts unique Order IDs; Profit Margin is Profit divided by Sales.

4. Read the charts: The trend shows monthly sales; the other charts compare sales across product categories, customer segments and regions.

Data & calculation notes

For accurate reporting, upload the original CSV. Column names are matched without case sensitivity. Dates are parsed from the Order Date field and numeric values from Sales and Profit.

Demo values are illustrative and are not a replacement for the original Superstore records. The previously discussed reference totals were approximately 2.30M sales, 286.40K profit, 5K orders and 12.47% margin; actual values depend on the uploaded file and filters.
